# Home calculator rule review — September 23, 2026

Scope: Atlantic City Electric, New Jersey residential heat pump water heater; one planned unit. This is a preliminary screening estimate, not utility approval or live funding verification.

Official sources inspected:
- https://homeenergysavings.atlanticcityelectric.com/residential/rebates/hvac-efficiency-program
- https://homeenergysavings.atlanticcityelectric.com/sites/default/files/60062_ACE_HVAC_Application_v4_RELEASE_508.pdf

Base rate: $750, limited to equipment cost (application page 4, water heater table; page 6, terms 5). Equipment must be ENERGY STAR certified. Installation period ends June 30, 2027; submit within 120 days of installation. Participating contractor and residential utility account required. Newly constructed homes excluded. Do not duplicate a utility incentive.

The app additionally withholds dollar calculations when model, certification answer, replacement type, building status, quantity, other-incentive answer, valid price/date or contractor confirmation is missing. These answers are customer supplied and do not constitute independent verification. Multiple units, conversions, like-for-like heat pump replacement and incentive combinations are routed for review; they are not automatically declared ineligible.

No model database lookup, income bonus, tax benefit, stacking, energy savings or completed-installation award calculation is implemented here. Webpage and PDF disagree on some promotional end dates for other equipment; no promotional rate was added. Business rates were not reverified in this change.

Review gate: October 23, 2026. An expired review gate hides the supported dollar estimate pending a fresh review. This is not an automatic online refresh.

Validation: 67 automated checks passed, TypeScript check and local preview build passed. Browser verified missing information and new-construction review states. Source matching is not a substitute for validation against actual approved utility applications; no actual award dataset was available.
