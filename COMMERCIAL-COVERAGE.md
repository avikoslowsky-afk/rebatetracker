# Nationwide commercial and multifamily search — current status

Updated October 22, 2026. All 50 states and DC have at least one official-source program pathway or research route. The catalog contains 65 entries; one legacy Con Edison commercial entry is withheld for source review. This is a nationwide starting catalog, not every utility or every commercial/multifamily program.

The four-step questionnaire covers location, building, delivery service and project. Optional building details are expandable. It saves selected answers into a private application after sign-in. Nationwide source links are visible at /business-rebates/states. Separate audience labels identify business pathways versus researched multifamily routes. A source listed for a business does not establish that a multifamily project qualifies; research links remain available where automatic matching is not established.

Source records live in lib/business-rebates.ts, lib/national-business-programs.ts and lib/all-state-business-sources.ts. Each record links to the relevant official utility, government or program administrator page. No paid data feed was added.

Main results exclude nonmatching, known-expired and source-review entries. Sources require review by October 29, 2026; after that, stale entries are withheld until the source dates are updated through an actual review. This is not an automatic live funding feed. Program funding can change between checks and must be confirmed with the administrator. No eligibility, reservation or award is guaranteed by screening.

Checks include the applicable unit-count, area, story, heating/service, rate class, annual-use, highest-demand, account-count, project timing and prior-incentive constraints implemented for individual pathways. New research routes remain needs-review until the exact service, account and technical conditions are confirmed. Additional technical criteria can exist in the full official application. No equipment-specific cash amount is invented.

Examples of conservative exclusions: APS exhausted existing-facility funds are not an available match; Evergy Missouri waitlist is not used as an available offer; expired Pacific Power California intake and expired seasonal bonuses are not promoted. Chugach charging credits are labelled utility bill credits, not cash. Loans, tax benefits, instant discounts and paid cash rebates must not be conflated in service billing.

The current source directory includes restricted local service areas, such as TVA/local power companies, participating Ohio municipal communities, and utility-specific Alaska charging. Selecting a state or ZIP does not verify an address or service account. New York detailed screening remains NYC-focused. All states can request project research, with no promised discovery outcome or turnaround.
