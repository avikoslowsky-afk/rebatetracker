RebateHarbor account and provider setup

EMAIL/PASSWORD ACCOUNTS
Registration and login are implemented with salted scrypt password hashes, signed HttpOnly cookies, same-origin checks, per-IP/account throttling and private customer-owned records.
Passwords require 15-128 characters. Signup shows a one-time recovery code. Password reset uses the saved code, rotates it and invalidates existing email-account sessions.
Email delivery and mailbox verification are not connected. Customers must save their recovery code. An email/password account cannot act as the configured team email; team reviewers use verified Google identity.
Required server configuration: AUTH_SESSION_SECRET (random 32+ characters), AUTH_ORIGIN (exact origin; HTTPS in production). D1 migration 0004 must be applied.
Local settings are configured in the ignored .dev.vars file. Do not copy local settings or test records into a source archive.

GOOGLE (OPTIONAL)
Configure GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID and GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET and register AUTH_ORIGIN/api/auth/google/callback. Google is hidden until configured. Google credentials are still not configured.

REBATE DATA
REWIRING_AMERICA_API_KEY is configured privately locally and saved as a hosting secret. Real HTTP 200 requests through the local site were verified for utilities, program discovery and household screening on 2026-10-21.
